## Store listing

Short description:

> Live NYC comedy lineups, favorites, sold-out status, and show reminders.

Full description:

> Every NYC comedy show. Filtered your way.
>
> Tonight NYC pulls live lineups from the Comedy Cellar, The Stand, major theaters, and dozens more—all in one app.
>
> WHAT YOU CAN DO
> • Browse tonight's lineups across NYC comedy venues
> • Mark comedians as Favorites or Skips
> • Get an on-device reminder before shows with a favorite comedian
> • See sold-out status before tapping through
> • Browse hundreds of comedians with bios and headshots
> • Track major arena and theater shows months ahead
> • Filter by venue, date, comedian, and availability
> • Share your filtered lineup with friends
>
> No account required. Favorites, skips, and reminders stay on your device. No ads or tracking.
>
> Built by an NYC comedy fan who got tired of checking multiple sites every Friday night.

## Console answers

- App access: all functionality is available without special access.
- Ads: no.
- Target audience: 13–15, 16–17, and 18+; not designed for children.
- Data safety: no data collected and no data shared. Native-shell analytics are disabled; preferences and notifications stay on device.
